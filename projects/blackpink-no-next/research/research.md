## E. The five variables (the thesis — interpretation, graded as such)

1. **A patient, rich label willing to starve its act** — 2016 YG could; 2020s quarterly economics cannot.
2. **The YouTube monoculture (2016–2020)** — one platform, one global feed; fragmented since.
3. **Luxury's first K-pop land grab** — the ambassador slots are now permanently held (largely by BLACKPINK themselves).
4. **Festival gatekeepers' one "first"** — Coachella headlined once; "first" cannot be re-spent.
5. **Four members whose individual gravity exceeds the label** — the modern endgame produces solo companies, not renewed groups.

All five existed simultaneously exactly once. The formula didn't break —
the world it compiled on was deprecated.
